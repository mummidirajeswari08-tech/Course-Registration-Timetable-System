Academic Architecture

Subjects Used & Integration

DBMS (Database Management)

Unit 1 & Unit 2 - Relational Schema

  • ER Modeling of Student, Course, Faculty, Sections & Enrollments.
  • Integrity constraints: Primary keys, Foreign keys, and CHECK rules.
  • Recursive CTE queries for complete prerequisite tree traversal.

DMGT & ADSA

Discrete Math & Advanced Data Structures

  • DMGT: Prerequisite propositional logic, relations, and transitivity.
  • ADSA: Course dependencies modeled as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG).
  • Kahn's Topological Sorting algorithm for graduation curriculum sequence.

OOPJ (Object Oriented Java)

Registrar Validation Engine

  • Validates prerequisite DAGs and detects schedule clashes before enrollment.
  • 3-color DFS cycle detector prevents circular prerequisite deadlocks.
  • Interval clash checking: (StartA < EndB) && (StartB < EndA).

Python ML Engine

Predictive Student Analytics

  • Computes weighted retention score from attendance % and GPA.
  • Auto-escalates students under 75% attendance or 2.2 GPA to HIGH RISK.
  • Dispatches automated incident reports and suggested interventions to advisors.
